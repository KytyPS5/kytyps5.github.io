The game runs with very low framerates. There are times when the game reaches 50fps but more times than not is stuck around 30 fps. Audio is very choppy in certain moments and due to the very low framerate it may stop in certain moments until the game continues running.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open KytyPS5
2. Boot Game 
3. Choose Profile
4. New Game

## Expected behavior

The game seems highly unstable, as there are lots of instances where the game may freeze or even crash due to failing to load certain objects in some areas. The fix for that is to reopen the game.

Other times, the controls seem to not work at all. For example there is a chance the right stick may not work at all at the beginning of the game, thus soft locking the player because you cannot get the Data Diver from its respective spot.
