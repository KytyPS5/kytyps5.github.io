The game reaches the main menu. The "rain drops" in the main menu turn the graphics black during its animation. Game boots all the way to the prologue. Game also seems to crash once booted into the Wayne Manor Backyard. The FPS is very low averaging what looks to be 5-15 FPS.

## Steps to reproduce

1.Open KytyPS5
2.Boot the game.
3.Progress through all 1st time startup terms and conditions. 
4. Main Menu will show the issues mentioned above.
5. click start, new game, and it should boot into "prologue" where you can observe the low FPS.

## Expected behavior

Game is super slow and laggy and will crash once in prologue.
