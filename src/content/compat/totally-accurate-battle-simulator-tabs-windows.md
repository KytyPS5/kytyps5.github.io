1. Open KytyPS5.
2. Boot Totally Accurate Battle Simulator (PPSA06081).
3. Enter a map (e.g., Tribal 2), place units, and start a battle.
4. Play normally and test freecam to observe the FPS and minor stutters.

## Expected behavior

- Performance: Averages around 39 FPS during heavy scenes, reaches 60–74 FPS during normal gameplay, and rarely spikes up to 120 FPS when looking up at the sky in freecam.
