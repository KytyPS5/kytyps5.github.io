The in-game tutorial in Lara's home that teaches the player how to jump doesn't progress.

**Steps to reproduce:** 1. Enter Lara's Home from the main menu 2. Proceed to the music room 3. After Lara says to press the jump button, try to jump. No matter how or which way you jump, the tutorial will not progress.
