## Steps to reproduce

1. Enter Lara's Home from the main menu
2. Proceed to the music room
3. After Lara says to press the jump button, try to jump. No matter how or which way you jump, the tutorial will not progress.

## Expected behavior

After pressing the jump button, the game should move onto explaining forward, backwards, and sideways jumps. (I show a comparison to the PC version down below that demonstrates what should happen.)
