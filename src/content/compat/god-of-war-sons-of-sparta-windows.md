In the boss fight with Metallon Bull, after Metallon Calf's health drops to half, Metallon Bull supposed to break through the right-hand door and join the boss fight, but this doesn't happen. This means the game soft-locked after Metallon Calf dies. I'm not sure if this is related to the emulator or an in-game issue.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open KytyPS5
2. Boot game
3. Progress the game
4. Coming to Metallon Bull place
5. The chase happens
6. Metallon Calf fight starts
7. Metallon Calf health drops half Metallon Bull health bar appear but he doesn't come to screen
8. Metallon Calf dies
9. Game soft-locks

## Expected behavior

When Metallon Calf health drops half Metallon Bull suppossed to appear in right of the screen but he doesn't
