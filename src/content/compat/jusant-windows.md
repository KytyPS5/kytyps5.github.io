The game launches, and the menu works, but there are visual glitches during the opening cutscenes and in gameplay. The game crashes after about 1 minute.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Launch KytyPS5
2. Launch the game
3. You'll start to notice the first graphical artifacts/glitches.
4. After the intro cutscene, during the first climb, the game will crash.

## Expected behavior

1. A clean image without graphical artifacts.
2. The ability to play without the emulator crashing
