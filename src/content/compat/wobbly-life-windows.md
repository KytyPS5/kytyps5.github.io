Wobbly Life successfully launches and reaches controllable gameplay on KytyPS5.

However, the game experiences significant FPS drops when entering indoor areas and tunnels.

These performance drops cause noticeable stuttering and negatively affect the overall gameplay experience.

Although the game remains playable, performance becomes inconsistent in these specific locations.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Launch KytyPS5.
2. Start Wobbly Life (PPSA29413).
3. Load a saved game or start a new game.
4. Enter the game world and begin playing normally.
5. Move into an indoor area or building.
6. Observe the significant FPS drops and stuttering.
7. Enter a tunnel and observe similar performance issues.

## Expected behavior

The game should maintain a reasonably stable frame rate when entering indoor areas and tunnels.

Gameplay should remain smooth and responsive without significant FPS drops or excessive stuttering.

## Extra notes

Performance issues appear to be location-dependent, particularly in indoor environments and tunnels.

Despite these FPS drops, the game remains playable and does not completely freeze.

The attached gameplay recording demonstrates the reported performance issues.

Tested on KytyPS5 using an AMD Ryzen 5 8600G, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5050, and 16 GB of DDR5 RAM.
