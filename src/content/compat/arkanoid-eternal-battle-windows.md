The game only works in local play.

## Steps to reproduce

- Open KytyPS5
- Boot Game
- bypass the message  "You are not connected to the PlayStation Network" (press X)
- Select Retro or Neo .

## Expected behavior

The game loads correctly; saves work.

## Extra notes

A small error message appears at launch: "You are not connected to the PlayStation Network"
but you can simply bypass this by pressing the 'X' button on your controller.
The game launches without any issues, whether you choose the retro (arcade) version or the 'Neo' (remastered) version.
