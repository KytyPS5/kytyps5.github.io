The game boots successfully, reaches the main menu, and enters playable gameplay. Controls and gameplay are working, and the game can be played. However, performance is extremely low during gameplay. The frame rate fluctuates between approximately 2 and 6 FPS, making the game very difficult to play at normal speed. No other major issues were observed during the test.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Start KytyPS5.
2. Launch Deliver At All Costs.
3. Wait for the game to reach the main menu.
4. Start a new game.
5. Continue until controllable gameplay begins.
6. Play normally and observe the frame rate.

The game reaches playable gameplay successfully, but performance remains very low at approximately 2–6 FPS.

## Expected behavior

The game should run at a normal and stable frame rate during gameplay without severe performance drops.
